# NaijaMart Business Insights

## 1. Revenue is concentrated in Laptops and Televisions

Laptops and Televisions are NaijaMart's leading revenue-generating categories, contributing approximately 35% and 27% of total revenue respectively. Combined, they account for about 62% of total revenue, indicating significant sales concentration in these two categories. Management should maintain adequate inventory levels for these high-performing categories while closely monitoring their profit margins to ensure that strong revenue performance translates into sustainable profitability.

## 2. Revenue remained relatively stable throughout the year

Monthly revenue remained relatively consistent throughout the year, with each month generating more than ₦2 billion in sales. This indicates stable demand and suggests that NaijaMart is not heavily dependent on a particular peak sales period. Management can leverage this consistency to improve inventory planning, cash-flow forecasting and resource allocation, while identifying opportunities to strengthen performance during comparatively weaker months.

## 3. Home Appliances and Laptops have the strongest profit margins

Home Appliances and Laptops recorded the strongest category-level profit margins at approximately 25%, indicating stronger profitability relative to revenue. In contrast, Televisions, despite being the second-highest revenue-generating category, recorded a lower profit margin of approximately 23.7%. Management should review the pricing and cost structure of the Television category to identify opportunities for margin improvement.

## 4. High revenue does not necessarily translate into high profitability

Among the five highest-revenue products, Sony Television 17 generated the highest revenue at approximately ₦854 million but recorded a relatively low profit margin of 19.2%. In comparison, Sony TV 52 and Lenovo Laptop generated lower revenues but achieved substantially higher profit margins of approximately 32.7% and 30.3% respectively. This demonstrates that high sales value does not necessarily translate into superior profitability. Management should therefore evaluate the pricing and cost structures of high-revenue, low-margin products.

## 5. Revenue is not heavily concentrated among the largest customers

The top five customers generated approximately ₦782.5 million, representing about 2.7% of NaijaMart's total revenue. This indicates that the business is not heavily dependent on a small number of major customers, suggesting a relatively diversified customer base and low customer concentration risk. Management can focus on retaining existing customers while continuing to expand the customer base.
